Why Horn Pollution Matters ?

Understanding the invisible threat

📊
By the Numbers
Noise above 85dB causes permanent hearing damage. City traffic horns regularly exceed 110dB — equivalent to a jackhammer at close range.
🧠
Health Impact
Chronic exposure to horn pollution elevates cortisol, disrupts sleep cycles, and increases cardiovascular risk by up to 20% in urban residents.
🛡️
SONET Solution
Smart acoustic dampening panels analyze, intercept, and cancel horn frequencies in real-time — protecting zones like hospitals, schools, and homes.
🌐
Network Effect
Each SONET unit can cover upto kms and build a network in a metropolitan city.
