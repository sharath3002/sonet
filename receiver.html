Inside the Technology

RF Signal Processing

At the core of the SONET RX lies a radio signal receiving unit that detects the following frequency range of 867 MHz which can be legally incorporated in India. Also this can be customized according to country's standard like USA, & other European nations.

Designed for Smart Cities

The RX module is engineered to support traffic systems, autonomous vehicles, and connected infrastructure. Its adaptive communication protocol ensures seamless data exchange across multiple endpoints.

Modular Hardware Architecture

The modular structure allows easy upgrades into vehicles and less maintenance cost, with standard adaptation cables and cords.






The Receiver module also called as RX in electronics domain, will bring a revolution in the horn system line. This will sit in the bonnet of a car horn-system , also inside the scooter, bikes, trucks ,bus etc. This will communicate with the nearby TX installed poles in public and takes control of the horn-system , just by turning off the horn switch. Even the driver or rider wishes to horn inside the TX zone, the horn doesn't work until and unless the vehicle is out of the TX zone. The TX Zone may be varying depending on the site or the perimeter of the School / Hospital / Tech Parks. It uses LoRa (Long Range) signals to communicate with each other. The TX will be actively transmitting the RF signals , whereas RX detects only if it is in the near zone.

During the installations, the RX module comes with the industry standard plug lines. It can be easily connected by a person or a service executive. Over the RX module shown in the above image, it will be covered with an enclosure for safety and reliability factors.



NOTE: Automobile manufacturers can come forward to make this happen by incorporating this technology in upcoming vehicles mandatorily ,which helps in contributing in building a smart city.
